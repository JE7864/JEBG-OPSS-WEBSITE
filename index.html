Our solution

Intelligent SOPs.
Operational confidence.

Designed to help reduce human error under stressful conditions and strengthen procedural compliance.

◈Real-time procedure surveillanceCompare actual process events with approved SOP steps, timing and conditions.
◈Visual SOP StudioConfigure sequences, parallel branches and hold points using DCS, PI and SCADA tags.
◈Intelligent SOP RecorderRecord tag transitions from start to stop and generate editable SOP drafts.
◈Automatic deviation detectionIdentify missing, delayed, premature and out-of-sequence events.
◈Historical execution replayReview synchronized process trends and event timelines.
◈Detailed deviation reportsCompare actual procedures against standard operating procedures.
